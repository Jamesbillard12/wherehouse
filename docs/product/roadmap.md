# Product roadmap

This roadmap describes longer-term evolution. The short-horizon path to the first tag is the
[MVP execution plan](mvp-execution-plan.md); release evidence belongs in the
[checklist](mvp-release-checklist.md), not here.

## MVP foundation

Reliable web/mobile inventory and hierarchy, useful search, reusable physical identifiers,
offline/idempotent synchronization, recoverable backups, supported Pi operation, and reusable
application capabilities. These foundations precede external mutation adapters and richer features.

## Early post-MVP household features

Lightweight categories/tags, checkout/return, movement/activity history, richer member roles,
advanced backup retention and NAS/S3/B2/OneDrive/Google Drive providers, exports, improved Pi
distribution and one-command installation. Prioritize observed household needs and data safety.

## Future intelligence and interfaces

After capability authorization, attribution/audit, reusable confirmation, and idempotency are ready:
optional provider-neutral AI recognition and assistance; natural-language queries; MCP; assistants;
automation; and controlled generative UI. All are adapters over the same application capabilities;
none becomes a second backend or required core dependency.

## Spatial evolution

Migrate toward unified arbitrary-depth locations only when product needs justify it, preserving IDs
and compatibility. Geometry/scanning, digital twins, spatial anchors, 3D/AR finding, occupancy,
packing and fit intelligence follow stable logical entities and always retain a non-spatial path.

## Advanced infrastructure

Microservices, Kubernetes, brokers, workflow engines, fleet management and enterprise controls wait
for demonstrated operational need. A Pi-suitable modular monolith remains the default.
